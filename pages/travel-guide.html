Travel Guide

Know the basics before you inquire.

Use this guide as general planning information. Your final route, permits, services and costs must be confirmed for your travel dates.

Best seasons

Spring and autumn are common trekking seasons. Upper Mustang can be suitable in selected monsoon months because of its rain-shadow location.

Permits

Permit needs vary by route and protected area. Restricted regions such as Manaslu and Upper Mustang need special documentation.

Altitude

Walk slowly, hydrate, follow acclimatization plans and tell your guide immediately if symptoms appear.

Packing

Bring layers, broken-in boots, rain protection, sun protection, personal medication and travel insurance suitable for your route.

Transport

Mountain flights and roads are weather and condition dependent. Build flexibility into your schedule.

Pricing

Prices are listed as Contact for Price until Hike X Nepal confirms group size, services, permits, transport and accommodation.
