Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This explains what information Hike X Nepal collects through this website and how it is used.

What we collect

When you submit the booking inquiry form, we collect your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, country, travel date, number of travelers, selected package and any message you enter. If you contact us directly by email, phone or WhatsApp, we receive whatever information you choose to share in that conversation.

We do not use cookies or tracking scripts on this site at this time. If that changes in the future, this page will be updated to reflect it.

How we use it

We use the information you provide only to respond to your inquiry, discuss trip details, and confirm bookings. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Where it is processed

The booking form is processed through Netlify, our website hosting provider, which delivers submissions to our email. If you message us via WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's (Meta's) own privacy practices, not ours, once it leaves this website.

How long we keep it

Inquiry details are kept only as long as needed to plan and complete your trip, or to respond to your questions, after which they may be deleted. You can request earlier deletion at any time using the contact details below.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request that it be deleted. Contact us at hikexnepal@gmail.com to make any of these requests.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. Family trips can of course be booked by a parent or guardian on a child's behalf.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
